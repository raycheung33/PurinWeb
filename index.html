Purin Cute Expense Tracker

A cute, private way to keep track of what you spend, with a smiling pudding and a sticker for everything.

ADD AN EXPENSE IN FOUR TAPS

  • How much? Type the amount on a built-in calculator, so you can add up a receipt or split a bill as you go.
  • What for? Pick a category, each with its own sticker. Your most recent ones come first.
  • Paid with? Cash, card, bank transfer, e-wallet, or a source you name yourself.
  • When? Today by default, or any other day, with an optional note.

SEE WHERE IT WENT

  • By day and by month, with a total for each.
  • By category, with a pie chart and each category’s share, for a month, a period you choose, or all time.
  • By source, so you can see what went on each card or in cash.
  • Search by note, category, source, amount or date.

MORE THAN ONE CURRENCY

Every expense keeps its own currency. Totals are shown separately for each currency and are never added together or converted, so the numbers are always exactly what you spent.

MAKE IT YOURS

  • Rename categories and payment sources, or combine two into one.
  • A daily reminder to record the day’s spending, at a time you pick.
  • Lock the app with a password, and unlock with your fingerprint.
  • Export everything to a file, and import it again on another phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your expenses are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. The app only goes online for Google Play, when you choose to buy the optional Purin Club membership.

PURIN CLUB

The app is free to use. Purin Club is an optional one-time purchase that gives you a membership card with your own member number. It is not needed for any of the tracking features.
